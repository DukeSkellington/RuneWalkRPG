# Rune Walk visual style

An old forest kingdom in the hour before sunrise. The traveler wears a copper
cloak, ivory scarf and dark boots. Runes are warm light against cold masonry.
Bracken's roofs echo the cloak; Glassmere uses silver-blue stone and black voids.

- Background palette 0: near-black outlines, pine shadow, fern midtone, sage light.
- Background palette 1: charcoal, slate, silver, pale moonlight for stone and UI.
- Background palette 2: deep blue, lake blue, shimmering pale water.
- Background palette 3: umber, copper, amber for paths, roofs and runes.
- Sprite palettes give the traveler copper/ivory and the keeper moss/linen.
- Terrain uses 16×16 metatiles, with four palette quadrants per 32×32 attribute
  block. Trees have clustered needle shapes, offset highlights and dark roots.
  Grass uses sparse tufts and flowers rather than a regular dotted grid.
- Mountains use broken stepped silhouettes and shaded faces. Water has spaced
  ripple lines and a hardware palette shimmer. Bridges use rails and crossbeams.
- Buildings have steep copper roofs, silver stonework and recessed doors.
  Ruins repeat broken columns and rune marks, with deliberate open corridors.
- The hero is 16×16, with four directions and a standing plus three walking
  poses per direction. Keep facial features readable at 1× scale.
- The original 5×7 uppercase font uses a consistent one-pixel rhythm. Framed
  menus use thin stone borders, generous spacing and restrained amber accents.
- The title logo is bespoke beveled pixel lettering over moonlit water and a
  ruined skyline. Star sprites and water shimmer supply subtle movement.

Editable sources live in `assets/` and the Python converters in `tools/`. No
Nintendo, commercial-game, stock pixel-art or downloaded music assets are used.
